# ARS Digital - Daily Action Plan

## Your Daily 30-Minute Routine (Non-Negotiable)

Do this EVERY DAY for the next 30 days. It takes 30 minutes max.

### Morning (15 min) — OUTREACH

#### Step 1: Find 5 businesses (5 min)
Go to ONE of these places and find 5 small businesses:
- **Google Maps**: Search "[city] + restaurant/clinic/real estate" → look for ones with bad or no websites
- **Facebook Groups**: Join local business groups → find people asking for help
- **LinkedIn**: Search "founder" or "owner" + your city
- **Instagram**: Search local hashtags (#LahoreBusiness, #KarachiFoodies, etc.)

#### Step 2: Send 5 messages (10 min)
- Copy the right template from `linkedin_messages.md` or `whatsapp_messages.md`
- Replace the [PLACEHOLDERS] with their actual details
- Send it
- Log it in `lead_tracker.md`

**That's it. 5 messages. Every single day.**

### Evening (15 min) — CONTENT

#### Step 3: Post 1 thing on social media (15 min)
Pick ONE caption from `social_media_captions.md` and post it. Rotate platforms:
- Monday → LinkedIn
- Tuesday → Twitter/X  
- Wednesday → Instagram
- Thursday → LinkedIn
- Friday → Twitter/X
- Saturday → Instagram (Reel if possible)
- Sunday → Rest or batch-create next week's content

## The Math (Why This Works)

| Metric | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Messages sent | 5 | 35 | 150 |
| Expected reply rate (10%) | 0.5 | 3-4 | 15 |
| Expected calls (30% of replies) | - | 1 | 4-5 |
| Expected sales (25% of calls) | - | - | 1-2 |

**1-2 service clients per month at even PKR 15,000-50,000 each = PKR 15,000-100,000/month**

That is far more than selling ebooks at PKR 280-500.

## Rules

1. **Never send the website link first.** Lead with value. Offer a free audit or observation.
2. **Follow up after 3 days** if no reply. One polite follow-up is fine.
3. **Don't pitch features. Pitch outcomes.** Not "I'll build you an automation" → "I'll save you 2 hours every day."
4. **Track everything.** If you don't measure it, you can't improve it.
5. **Consistency beats perfection.** A bad message sent is better than a perfect message unsent.
