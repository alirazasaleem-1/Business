## 💡 WhatsApp Delivery Rules for Best Response Rates:
1. **Never send links in message #1:** WhatsApp algorithms flag unsolicited links as spam. Always ask permission first ("Can I send the screenshot/video?"), then send the link once they reply.
2. **Use voice notes for follow-up:** If they reply with interest, reply with a 20-second friendly, enthusiastic voice note. It builds massive trust instantly.
3. **Set up WhatsApp Business:** Ensure your profile has your logo, bio (*"AI Automation & Websites for Small Businesses"*), and catalogue link.
