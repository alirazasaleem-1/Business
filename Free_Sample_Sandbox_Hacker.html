Why You Need a Cyber Lab

If you want to learn how hackers break into systems, you can't just read about it—you have to practice. But practicing on your own home network or public websites is incredibly dangerous (and illegal).

That is where a Cyber Lab comes in.

A cyber lab (or sandbox) is a simulated, isolated environment running entirely within your current computer. If you accidentally download malware or break a system in your lab, it cannot escape and harm your real computer. You just click "Reset" and start over.

Installing VirtualBox

To build a lab, we need a Hypervisor. This is software that lets you run multiple "virtual computers" (Virtual Machines or VMs) inside your main computer.

We will use Oracle VM VirtualBox because it is powerful and 100% free.

Step-by-Step Installation:

  1. Go to virtualbox.org/wiki/Downloads.
  2. Click on "Windows hosts" (or macOS/Linux depending on your system).
  3. Run the installer and click "Next" through the default options.
  4. Once installed, open VirtualBox. You are now ready to add virtual machines!
You may need to enable Hardware Virtualization (VT-x / AMD-V) in your computer's BIOS settings if VirtualBox gives you an error when starting a machine.
